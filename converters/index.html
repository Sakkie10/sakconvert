Standards & Accuracy

SakConvert uses internationally recognized conversion factors as defined by:

  • SI (International System of Units) — BIPM 2019 (global metric standard)
  • NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology) — exact conversion constants and SP 811 / SP 330
  • ISO / IEC standards — especially for digital units (e.g. KiB, MiB) and engineering
  • CODATA 2022 — recommended physical constants (when applicable)
  • Traditional & Historical Definitions — for Imperial, US Customary, nautical, and legacy units

All conversions are based on official reference tables used in engineering textbooks, scientific handbooks, and metrology laboratories.
