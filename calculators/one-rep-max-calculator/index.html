Formula

1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)

This calculator uses the Epley formula, commonly used in strength training and powerlifting.

What is One-Rep Max?

Your one-rep max (1RM) is the maximum amount of weight you can lift for a single repetition of an exercise.

Why athletes use 1RM

Strength athletes and coaches use 1RM estimates to measure progress, structure training blocks, and determine percentages for workouts.

Training insight

Most people test strength too often. Quietly, consistent submaximal training usually builds strength faster over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a 1RM calculator?

It provides a good estimate, especially for lower rep ranges. Actual strength varies between individuals.

What rep range works best?

Most formulas are more accurate between 1–10 repetitions.

Should beginners test true maxes?

Most beginners benefit more from estimated maxes than attempting dangerous true one-rep lifts.
