Formula

Body Mass Index is calculated using weight and height:

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

BMI is a simple screening measure that compares body weight to height. It can help estimate whether a person is underweight, within a healthy weight range, overweight, or obese.

Understanding BMI

Body Mass Index, commonly known as BMI, is a widely used measure that compares your weight to your height. It is often used as a quick guide to understand whether your body weight is within a general healthy range.

BMI categories

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9: Healthy weight
  • 25.0 to 29.9: Overweight
  • 30.0 and above: Obese

How to calculate BMI

Convert your height from centimetres into metres, square that number, then divide your weight in kilograms by the squared height value. For example, someone who is 180cm tall and weighs 75kg has a BMI of around 23.1.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a simple calculation that compares your weight to your height.

What is a healthy BMI?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered to be within the healthy weight range.

Is BMI always accurate?

No. BMI does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, bone density, or athletic build. It is best used as a general screening tool rather than a complete measure of health.

Can athletes have a high BMI?

Yes. Athletes or people with higher muscle mass may have a higher BMI even if they have a healthy body composition.
