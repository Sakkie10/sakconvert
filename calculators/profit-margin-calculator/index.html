Formula

Profit margin shows what percentage of your selling price is profit.

Profit Margin = (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100

Profit is calculated by subtracting your cost price from your selling price.

What is profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of revenue left after subtracting costs. It helps businesses understand how much money they actually keep from each sale.

Example calculation

If a product costs £50 and sells for £80, the profit is £30. The profit margin is 37.5%, because £30 is 37.5% of the £80 selling price.

Profit margin vs markup

Profit margin is based on the selling price. Markup is based on the cost price. This means margin and markup are related, but they are not the same thing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good profit margin?

A good profit margin depends on the industry. Some businesses operate on low margins with high volume, while others use higher margins with fewer sales.

Can profit margin be negative?

Yes. If your cost price is higher than your selling price, your profit margin will be negative because you are selling at a loss.

Why is profit margin important?

Profit margin helps you price products, compare performance, and understand whether your business is generating enough profit from sales.
