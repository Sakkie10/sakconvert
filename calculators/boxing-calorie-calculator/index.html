Formula

Calories = (MET × weight × 3.5 ÷ 200) × duration

Higher intensity boxing significantly increases calorie burn.

How many calories does boxing burn?

A 70kg person burns approximately 400–800 calories per hour depending on intensity.

How this calculator works

This tool uses MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) values to estimate calorie burn based on effort level.
