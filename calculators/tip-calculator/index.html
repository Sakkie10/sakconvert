Quick Tip Guide

Tip = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100)
Total = Bill + Tip
Per Person = Total ÷ People

15% = Good • 18-20% = Excellent • 10% = Average

What is a Tip Calculator?

It helps you quickly calculate the correct tip and split the bill fairly when eating out or using services.

How to Use This Calculator

Enter the total bill amount before tip, choose your desired tip percentage, and optionally enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator instantly shows the tip amount, total bill, and amount per person.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good tip percentage?

15–20% is standard for good to excellent service in most restaurants. 10% is acceptable for average service.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Most people tip on the pre-tax bill amount. Our calculator uses whatever bill amount you enter.

Can I round the tip?

Yes. Many people round up to the nearest dollar or to make the total a round number.

Does this work for other services?

Yes — restaurants, delivery, taxis, haircuts, and any service where tipping is customary.

What if the service was bad?

You can lower the tip percentage. Many people still leave 10% even for average service.

How do I split the bill unevenly?

Use the number of people field for equal splits. For uneven splits, calculate the total first and divide manually.
