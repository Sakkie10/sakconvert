Formula

To add VAT, multiply the net amount by the VAT rate, then add it to the original amount.

VAT Amount = Net Amount × VAT Rate

To remove VAT, divide the gross amount by 1 plus the VAT rate.

What is VAT?

VAT stands for value added tax. It is a tax added to many goods and services. Businesses often need to calculate VAT when preparing prices, quotes, invoices, and receipts.

How to add VAT

To add VAT, multiply the original amount by the VAT rate, then add the VAT amount to the original price.

How to remove VAT

To remove VAT from a VAT-inclusive price, divide the total price by 1 plus the VAT rate. This gives the price before VAT.

Example calculation

If the amount is £100 and the VAT rate is 20%, the VAT amount is £20. The total price including VAT is £120.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard VAT rate in the UK?

The standard UK VAT rate is commonly 20%, although some goods and services may use reduced or zero rates.

Can I use this calculator to remove VAT?

Yes. Select “Remove VAT from amount” to work backwards from a VAT-inclusive price.

Is VAT calculated on the net or gross amount?

VAT is usually calculated on the net amount. The gross amount is the final total after VAT has been added.
