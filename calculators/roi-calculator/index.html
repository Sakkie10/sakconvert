Formula

ROI shows how much return you made compared with the original amount invested.

ROI = (Net Profit ÷ Investment Cost) × 100

Net profit is calculated by subtracting the investment cost from the total return.

What is ROI?

ROI stands for return on investment. It helps measure whether an investment, campaign, product, or business decision made a profit compared with its cost.

Example calculation

If you spend £1,000 and receive £1,500 back, your net profit is £500. Your ROI is 50%, because £500 is half of the original £1,000 investment.

Why ROI matters

ROI is useful because it gives you a simple percentage for comparing different business opportunities. A higher ROI usually means a more efficient return, but risk and time should also be considered.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROI?

A good ROI depends on the industry, risk level, and time period. A small low-risk return may be good in one situation, while a high-risk project may need a much higher ROI to be worthwhile.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. ROI is negative when the total return is lower than the original investment cost.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Profit is the amount of money made. ROI expresses that profit as a percentage of the original investment.
