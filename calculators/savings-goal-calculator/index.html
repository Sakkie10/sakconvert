Savings Formula

Monthly Deposit = (Target − Current) × r / ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where r = monthly interest rate and n = total months.

What is a Savings Goal Calculator?

It calculates the fixed monthly amount you need to save to reach a target, taking advantage of compound interest.

How to Use This Calculator

Enter your target amount, current savings (if any), the number of years you have, and the expected annual return. The calculator shows the exact monthly deposit needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I already have some savings?

The calculator automatically reduces the monthly amount needed because your current savings will also grow with interest.

What interest rate should I use?

Use a realistic rate: 3–5% for savings accounts, 7–10% for long-term investments. Be conservative for shorter timeframes.

Can I reach my goal faster?

Yes — by increasing your monthly contribution, extending the timeline, or earning a higher return.

Does this include taxes or inflation?

No. This is a simplified calculator. For more accuracy, consider adjusting your target amount upward to account for inflation.

What if I miss a month of saving?

You can catch up by increasing future contributions or extending your timeline slightly.

Is this good for retirement planning?

Yes, it works well for both short-term and long-term goals like retirement, a house deposit, or a vacation fund.
