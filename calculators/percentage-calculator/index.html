What is a percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as part of 100. Percentages are used to compare values, calculate discounts, measure growth, and understand changes over time.

How to calculate a percentage of a number

To calculate a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the base number. For example, 20% of 500 is 100.

How to calculate percentage change

Percentage change shows how much a value has increased or decreased compared with its original value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20% of 500?

20% of 500 is 100. This is calculated by multiplying 500 by 0.20.

What does percentage increase mean?

Percentage increase means how much a number has grown compared with its original value.

What does percentage decrease mean?

Percentage decrease means how much a number has fallen compared with its original value.
