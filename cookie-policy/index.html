What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember preferences, measure performance, and improve user experience.

How SakConvert Uses Cookies

SakConvert may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor behaviour, improve performance, remember preferences, and support analytics or advertising services.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies help the website operate correctly and securely. These cookies may be required for basic site functionality.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use SakConvert, including which pages are visited and how the website performs.

Advertising Cookies

Advertising cookies may be used by advertising partners to deliver, personalise, or measure advertisements. These may be used if SakConvert displays ads in the future.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may help remember settings or choices you make while using the website.

Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services, such as analytics providers or advertising partners, may place cookies on your device when you use SakConvert.

These third parties are responsible for their own cookie and privacy practices.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, delete, block, or control cookies through browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some websites function.

SakConvert may use Google services such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to help provide, measure, and personalise these services.
